Material HN is a fast, offline-capable client for Hacker News.

FEEDS AND COMMENTS
Top, New, Best, Ask, Show and Jobs, plus full comment trees with collapsing,
in-thread navigation and favorites. Voting, replying, submitting and saving all
work from inside the app.

READER MODE
Open any linked article in a clean reader view with Readability. Choose the font,
size and line height, switch to a dark or pure-black theme, and read long
discussions without leaving the app.

OFFLINE
Read stories, comments and saved articles without a connection. Saved items and
pending votes or comments are queued and synced when the network returns.

SEARCH
Search stories and comments through the Algolia Hacker News index, and open
results directly in the thread.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no advertising, no tracking SDKs. Links are stripped of common
tracking parameters before they open, known ad and tracker domains are blocked,
and all traffic uses HTTPS. Login credentials are encrypted with a key that never
leaves your device and are excluded from device backups.

FREE SOFTWARE
