Privacy Policy — Material HN (in.rahulja.materialhn)

Material HN is a Hacker News client. It has no analytics, no advertising, no
crash-reporting service and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the app is
reported to the app's authors.

1. Data you provide

Logging in to Hacker News is optional. If you log in, the username is kept in
app preferences and the password is stored encrypted with a key held by the
Android Keystore; that key never leaves your device. Credentials are sent only
to news.ycombinator.com, and only when you perform an action that requires
them (voting, replying, submitting, saving).

You can remove the stored credentials at any time by uninstalling the app or
clearing its storage. Uninstalling removes all local data.

2. Data stored on your device

Reading history, saved stories, drafts, cached readability articles, cached
web pages and the sync queue are stored locally in an app-private database,
private preferences and the app cache. They are not transmitted anywhere.
Credentials and WebView data are excluded from Android's cloud backup and
device-transfer features.

3. Network connections

The app contacts the following services, and only these, plus the article site
you open:

- hacker-news.firebaseio.com — official Hacker News API
- hn.algolia.com — Algolia Hacker News search
- news.ycombinator.com — login and authenticated actions

Article URLs are opened through the platform browser or the in-app reader.
Links are stripped of common tracking parameters before they are opened. The
app blocks known advertising and tracker domains with a locally bundled
blocklist.

All connections use HTTPS. Cleartext HTTP is disabled in the app's network
security configuration.

4. Permissions

- Internet and network state: to fetch Hacker News content.
- Accounts (AUTHENTICATE_ACCOUNTS, GET_ACCOUNTS): to let you choose a saved
  account. The app cannot read or modify accounts that belong to other apps.
- Notifications: for background sync results and replies, only if you enable
  them.
- Sync settings: to schedule background sync of saved items.
- Vibrate: for haptic feedback.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

6. Changes

This policy is part of the source repository. Any change ships with the app
version that changes the behaviour.

7. Contact

Report issues at https://github.com/xRahul/materialisheep/issues.
